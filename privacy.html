AWBuddy Privacy Policy

Digi Product Factory · Effective 3 October 2026 (last updated 3 October 2026)

This policy explains what the AWBuddy Android app ("AWBuddy", "the app") collects, why, and what you can do about it. AWBuddy is made by Digi Product Factory, Chennai, India ("we", "us").

In short: the shipping labels you scan, and the customer details read from them, stay on your phone and are not sent to us. We store only what we need to sign you in, count your scans and manage your subscription.

1. Who AWBuddy is for

AWBuddy is a business tool for sellers who ship parcels. It is intended for users aged 18 or over, using it for business. It is not directed at children.

2. Data processed on your phone only

  • Camera images of shipping labels are analysed on the device (Google ML Kit on-device barcode and text recognition). Images are not stored or uploaded.
  • Scan records: AWB number, courier, customer name, phone, address, PIN code, product, amount, and the date and time of the scan. These are saved in a database on your phone and are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Excel exports are created on your phone. They leave the phone only when you share them yourself (for example by email or WhatsApp).

Saved scans are also left out of Android's cloud backup, so they don't go to Google's servers either. Google ML Kit may send Google general usage and performance metrics about the recognition feature. It does not send the label images or the text read from them.

You are responsible for handling your customers' details lawfully, as you would with any other shipping records.

4. Services we use

  • Firebase Authentication (Google): phone-number sign-in by SMS code.
  • Cloud Firestore (Google, Mumbai region): stores the account, counter and subscription data listed above.
  • Cloud Functions for Firebase (Google, Mumbai region): verifies purchases with Google Play and re-checks subscriptions daily. Its logs record your phone number, plan and the outcome of each check.
  • Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity: checks that requests come from the genuine AWBuddy app on a genuine device. Google Play Integrity processes device and app integrity signals for this.
  • Google Play Billing: subscriptions are bought and paid for through Google Play. We never see your card, UPI or bank details. Google's own privacy policy applies to your payment.
  • Google ML Kit: on-device barcode and text recognition (see section 2).

These services are provided by Google and may process data on servers outside India. See the Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

5. Retention

  • Account, counter and subscription data: kept while your account exists, and deleted when you ask us to delete your account (see section 7).
  • Server logs: kept by Google Cloud Logging for its standard retention period (normally 30 days), then deleted automatically.
  • "Trial used" marker: after deletion we keep a one-way hash of your phone number, with no other details, so that the same number can't start a new free trial. It cannot be turned back into your number.
  • Google Play order records are kept by Google under its own policies.
  • Scans on your phone stay until you delete them in the app or uninstall it.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Our database rules let each signed-in user read only their own account record. Only our server can change plan and subscription details.

7. Deleting your account

You can ask us to delete your AWBuddy account and its data at any time. Step-by-step instructions are on the Delete your AWBuddy account page. In short:

  • Cancel any Google Play subscription first. Deleting your account does not cancel it.
  • Email digiproductfactory@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my AWBuddy account" and the phone number you sign in with.
  • We verify the request and delete your account data within 7 days.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing us. You can withdraw camera permission in Android settings at any time, although the app can't scan labels without it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above. For significant changes we'll also tell you in the app.
