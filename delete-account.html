Delete your AWBuddy account

AWBuddy by Digi Product Factory

Follow these steps to have your AWBuddy account and its data deleted.

  1. Cancel your Google Play subscription first
    Deleting your AWBuddy account does NOT cancel your subscription. Google Play keeps charging you until you cancel it there.
    Open the Play Store app → tap your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → AWBuddy → Cancel subscription. Or go to play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.
  2. Email us your request Send an email to digiproductfactory@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my AWBuddy account" and the mobile number you sign in to AWBuddy with.
    Email deletion request
  3. We verify and delete within 7 days We confirm that the request comes from the account holder (we may ask you to reply from the same email or confirm details of your account), then delete your account data within 7 days and email you when it's done.

What we delete

  • Your AWBuddy account record: phone number, sign-in identity (Firebase user ID), trial and scan counters, time of last scan and plan tier.
  • Subscription details we store: Google Play purchase token, plan, expiry, subscription state, auto-renew status and hashed billing account ID.
  • Our purchase records that link a purchase to your account.

What we keep

  • A "trial used" marker: a one-way hash of your phone number, with no other details, so that the same number can't start another free trial. It cannot be turned back into your number.
  • Google's own order records of any Google Play purchases, which Google keeps under its own policies.
  • Server logs that may contain your phone number are deleted automatically by Google Cloud Logging after its standard retention period (normally 30 days).

Scans stored on your phone

Your saved scans (customer names, addresses, AWB numbers and so on) are stored only on your phone, so we can't delete them for you. To remove them, delete them in the app's History screen or uninstall AWBuddy. If Android backup is on, also remove AWBuddy's data from your Google account backup.

More details: AWBuddy Privacy Policy → Deleting your account.
